# Code of Conduct

Aristo is a guild hall, not a clubhouse — we're here to do careful
work together, and that depends on everyone being able to take part
without harassment or condescension. We hold ourselves to the
Contributor Covenant (v2.1), included below.

Reports go to **conduct@aretta.ai** and are handled by the
maintainers. At least two people review every enforcement decision,
and we keep your report confidential.
