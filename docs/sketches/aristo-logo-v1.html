Design notes

What works: The two-tower + dipping-cable + deck silhouette is unambiguously a suspension bridge — survives at 16px. The omega reading is secondary but lands once you're looking for it (two legs, curved top, base feet). International Orange in context evokes Golden Gate immediately.

What might need tweaking: (a) Tower taper is very subtle (1px each side) — could exaggerate for clearer Art Deco character; (b) Cable dip depth is currently ~50% of tower height — bridge-accurate but could be reduced to read more "arch"-like; (c) Deck flange could be thicker for omega-foot emphasis vs. bridge-deck accuracy.

Variants to consider: Concept A (literal bridge with cable arching DOWN, this sketch); Concept B (cable arching UP, more omega-like, less bridge-accurate); Concept C (add a horizontal cross-brace strut on each tower for full Art Deco character at the cost of busier 16px rendering).
