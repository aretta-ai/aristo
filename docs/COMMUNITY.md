# Community

Aristo is in alpha, and we're building it in the open. Here's where
each kind of conversation goes.

- **Questions, ideas, feedback** → [Discussions](https://github.com/aretta-ai/aristo/discussions).
  This is what we want most right now — tell us where Aristo helps,
  where it gets in the way, and what you wish it did.
- **Bugs & feature requests** → [Issues](https://github.com/aretta-ai/aristo/issues).
- **Security issues** → [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md) — please report
  privately, not in a public issue.
- **Contributing code** → [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

However you show up, we hold to our [Code of
Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md): careful work, done with care for the
people doing it.

We respond as fast as a small team in alpha can — thanks for your
patience, and for being here early.
